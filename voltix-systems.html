About

Built around technology.
Driven by results.

Voltix Systems builds custom PCs and repairs the ones that stopped behaving. Two halves of the same job: understanding hardware well enough to specify it correctly, and well enough to find out what went wrong with it.

Every machine that comes through gets diagnosed before anything is quoted. Every build gets specified around the software it will actually run. We'd rather talk you out of a part you don't need than sell it to you.

The aim is simple — you leave knowing what your computer does, what was done to it, and what it will need next.
